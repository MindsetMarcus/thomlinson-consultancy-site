Why a mastermind

Work less, stress less, smile more.

So many business owners are struggling at the moment. Long days, constant pressure and a sense that you have to carry everything yourself. A mastermind changes that. You sit in a small group with other owners, you get coached, and you leave each week with clear commitments instead of a heavier head.

I have been running mastermind groups for years. I helped found the Directors Hub in Harrogate, where directors and business owners gave each other peer-to-peer support, and I ran small groups regularly until COVID. I want to bring them back for owners in Worthing and the surrounding area. I live here, I am active in the local community, including charity events for the Lions, and I would like to help local businesses thrive.

Every week

One hour that moves everything forward.

20 minutes

Learn

A teaching segment based on the iCan Thinking method: mindset, leadership, productivity, strategy and performance.

20 minutes

Get coached

Members send in questions beforehand and several are coached live in front of the group.

Commit

A collective accountability round where everyone commits to their goals for the week.

Questions

Quick answers.

Is there a business mastermind in Worthing?

I am starting local groups. The weekly sessions are online, and I intend each group of 12 to meet in person at least four times a year, with a quarterly meeting in Worthing and a social side too. Register your interest and I will keep you posted.

I am a sole trader. Is it for me?

Yes. Owners at many sizes get value from a peer group, as long as you want to grow and are willing to take part.

What happens in a session?

Each weekly session has three parts: a teaching segment, live coaching on members' questions, and an accountability round where everyone commits to their goals for the week.

How big are the groups?

Groups are capped at 12.

Good to know. Coaching is coaching and education. It is not therapy or medical treatment, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can also call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.

Questions

Quick answers.

How much time does it take?

One core hour a week, with optional engagement between sessions in the iCan app and community.

Who is it for?

Business owners, founders, SME owners, consultants and professional service providers who take growth and quality seriously and value personal responsibility.

How many people are in a group?

No more than 12, so everyone gets time to ask questions and be heard.

How long is the commitment?

Business mastermind groups are a 12 month engagement, paid monthly, because that is about how long it takes to put the work in.

How do I join?

Membership starts with a short introductory conversation to make sure it is a good fit for you and for the group.

What does it cost?

Current prices for every iCan level, including iCan Mastermind, are at icanthinking.com.

Good to know. Coaching is coaching and education. It is not therapy or medical treatment, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can also call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.

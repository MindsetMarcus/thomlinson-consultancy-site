About me and Worthing

Space to think at the top.

Running something significant is a different kind of pressure. Decisions carry weight, and it is rare to find someone you can talk to openly without the conversation affecting anyone else.

That is what executive coaching gives you. We start with a short Leadership Readiness Diagnostic and a 90 Minute Strategy Intensive, so you leave with a 90 day roadmap, then we meet weekly or fortnightly to work on the real decisions in front of you. I was one of the founding members of the Directors Hub, and I spent 25 years in financial services, becoming an IFA and growing my business from one office to five.

I live in Worthing and I am active in the community, including charity events for the Lions.

Questions

Quick answers.

Can we meet in person in Worthing?

Yes. I live in Worthing and meet clients here, as well as online.

Who is executive coaching for?

Directors, managing directors, partners and senior leaders who want a private sounding board.

What happens in the 90 Minute Strategy Intensive?

We identify your goals and you leave with a 90 day roadmap.

Is it confidential?

Our conversations are private. We agree how we work together, including confidentiality, at the start.

Good to know. Coaching is coaching and education. It is not therapy or medical treatment, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can also call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.

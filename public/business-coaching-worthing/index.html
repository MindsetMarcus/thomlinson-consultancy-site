About me and Worthing

Growing a business where you live.

Running a business in a town like Worthing often means wearing every hat: sales, delivery, accounts, people and the ideas. It is easy to get so busy keeping the business going that you never stop and ask where it is going.

Coaching gives you that space. With the iCan Thinking method we start with a clear picture of where your business is today, using the Business Health Check, then build a plan and the mindset to follow it. My background is in financial services, where I grew my business from one office to five, so I understand the numbers as well as the people.

I live in Worthing and I am getting involved in the community, including charity events for the Lions. I would like to help more local businesses thrive, and the people running them enjoy it more.

Questions

Quick answers.

Can we meet in person in Worthing?

Yes. I live in Worthing and meet clients here, as well as online. We agree a time and place when you book your discovery call.

I am a sole trader. Is business coaching worth it?

Often, yes. Many owner-managers find that a clear plan and regular accountability make the biggest difference, whatever their size.

How long does the programme last?

The iCan Grow programme runs for 12 months, with sessions weekly at first and fortnightly after about three months.

Is there a group option?

Yes. I am planning local business mastermind groups, and the weekly online group is open to owners from anywhere. See the business mastermind page for Worthing.

Good to know. Coaching is coaching and education. It is not therapy or medical treatment, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can also call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.

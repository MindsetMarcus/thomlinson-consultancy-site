About me and Brighton

A sounding board for senior leaders.

Directors, managing directors and partners often have everyone around them and no one to think out loud with. The people you lead cannot always hear your doubts, and your peers have their own agendas.

Executive coaching with me is a private, one to one conversation about what you are carrying and where you want to go. We look at your thinking, your decisions and the way you lead, and turn what we find into a clear 90 day roadmap. I am a member of the Institute of Directors, I was one of the founding members of the Directors Hub in Harrogate, and I spent 25 years in financial services.

I have lived in and around Brighton for more than five years and coach a group of clients here.

Questions

Quick answers.

What is the difference between executive coaching and leadership coaching?

Executive coaching is one to one and private, for senior people who want a sounding board. Leadership coaching is wider, and includes team programmes and workshops.

Do you meet executives in person in Brighton?

Yes, some. I am in Brighton one day a week, and many clients prefer online sessions.

What is the Leadership Readiness Diagnostic?

A 20 minute assessment of clarity, communication, cadence and calm, which gives us a starting point.

Is it confidential?

Our conversations are private. We agree how we work together, including confidentiality, at the start.

Good to know. Coaching is coaching and education. It is not therapy or medical treatment, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can also call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.

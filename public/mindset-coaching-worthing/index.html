Why mindset first

Change the thinking, and the rest follows.

Many of us carry a long list of things we know would help: more exercise, better organisation, time to rest, time to think. Knowing is not the problem. What is missing is the belief that it is worth it, and the habit of acting on it. Mindset coaching goes to that root.

Using the iCan Thinking method, we start with how you think, then back it with small daily actions, so the change shows up in everyday life rather than staying an idea. Once your mindset is working for you, coaching in any other area, from life to business, becomes far more effective.

I live in Worthing and I am part of the local community here, including charity events for the Lions. If you would like a coach who lives down the road, I would be glad to meet you.

Questions

Quick answers.

Can we meet in person in Worthing?

Yes. I live in Worthing and meet clients here, as well as online. We agree a time and place when you book your discovery call.

I am not sure what I want to change. Is mindset coaching still right?

Yes. Many people start with just a feeling that something is stuck. Finding out what is behind it is part of the work.

How is this different from other things I have tried?

Many approaches add more information. We change the mindset your thoughts and actions come from, then back it with small daily action, a coach and a community.

How much time does it take?

Sessions are typically weekly or fortnightly, plus a few minutes a day with your portal and the iCan app. Many actions take just five minutes.

Good to know. Coaching is coaching and education. It is not therapy or medical treatment, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can also call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.

Why a mastermind

Work less, stress less, smile more.

Busy London owners often have the least time to step back, which is exactly why a regular, structured hour matters. In a mastermind you get coaching, a peer group of up to 12 owners, and a weekly accountability rhythm that keeps you focused on the few things that move the business forward.

I am in London one day a week meeting clients, and I am part of a large networking group and business community in the city. I also know what peer support can do: I was one of the founding members of the Directors Hub, a peer-to-peer group for directors and business owners that started in Harrogate, and I ran small masterminds regularly before COVID. I am excited to run them again.

One hour that moves everything forward.

Questions

Quick answers.

Will the London mastermind meet in person?

That is the plan. The weekly sessions are online, and I intend each group of 12 to meet in person at least four times a year, with a quarterly meeting in London plus social lunches or dinners. Register your interest and I will update you.

I already network a lot. Why a mastermind?

Networking widens your contacts. A mastermind goes deeper, with the same small group every week, coaching on your real questions and accountability for your commitments.

How much time does it take?

One hour a week, plus the work you commit to between sessions.

How long is the commitment?

Business mastermind groups are a 12 month engagement, paid monthly.

Good to know. Coaching is coaching and education. It is not therapy or medical treatment, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can also call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.

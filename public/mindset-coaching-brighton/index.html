Why mindset first

Change the thinking, and the rest follows.

Most people do not need another list of things to do. You know you should plan your time, look after your health, exercise, meditate and speak to people more kindly, starting with yourself. The real question is why you do not do the things you already know. In my experience the answer is almost never information. It is the thinking underneath.

That is what mindset coaching with the iCan Thinking method changes. We work on the beliefs, habits of thought and inner voice that drive your choices, until you genuinely see the value of the things you want to do. Only then does any other kind of coaching, whether life, business or leadership, really take hold.

I have lived in and around Brighton for more than five years, spend a day a week in the city and coach a group of clients here, so this is work I do close to home.

Questions

Quick answers.

What is the difference between mindset coaching and life coaching?

Mindset coaching works on the thinking that drives your behaviour, so you want to do what you already know is good for you. Life coaching then builds on that with goals and plans across the different areas of your life. Many people start with mindset and move on to life, business or leadership coaching.

Do you meet in person in Brighton?

Yes, some clients. I am in Brighton one day a week, and most Brighton clients prefer online sessions. Either works.

How quickly will I notice a change?

Most people feel a shift in their thinking within the first few weeks. Deeper, lasting change builds over time as small daily wins add up.

Is this therapy?

No. Coaching is not therapy or medical treatment and does not replace professional help.

Good to know. Coaching is coaching and education. It is not therapy or medical treatment, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can also call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.

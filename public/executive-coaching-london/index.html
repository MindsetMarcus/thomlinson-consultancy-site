About me and London

Clarity for people who decide.

The more senior you are, the fewer people there are to think with. Colleagues have their own interests, and friends are not always close to the pressures of your role.

Executive coaching with me is built around you: a Leadership Readiness Diagnostic, a 90 Minute Strategy Intensive that ends in a 90 day roadmap, then regular one to one sessions on the decisions and relationships that matter most. I am a member of the Institute of Directors and spent 25 years in financial services, including growing my own firm from one office to five.

I am in London one day a week and part of a large networking group and business community in the city.

Questions

Quick answers.

Do you meet executives in person in London?

Yes, on my London day each week. I also coach online, which many senior people find easiest.

How is this different from leadership coaching?

Executive coaching is one to one and private. Leadership coaching is wider, and includes team programmes and workshops.

How much time does it take?

A short diagnostic and a 90 minute intensive to begin, then weekly or fortnightly sessions.

Is it confidential?

Our conversations are private. We agree how we work together, including confidentiality, at the start.

Good to know. Coaching is coaching and education. It is not therapy or medical treatment, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can also call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.

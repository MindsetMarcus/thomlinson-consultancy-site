iCan Speak

From terrified to terrific in a day.

I live in Worthing, and I would like more people here to feel confident speaking up, whether that is at a meeting, a networking event, a charity event or on a stage. My iCan Speak days, which have been hugely popular, have so far been held in Brighton, which is next door.

If you would rather learn with colleagues, I can also run an in-house day for your team. Teams of up to 12 can take the full day, or up to 30 can take an abridged version.

What you will learn.

Mindset and nerves reset

Breath, body and thought drills to settle your nerves fast.

Story structure

A repeatable arc, Know, Feel, Do, that makes any message land.

Hot-seat coaching

Deliver a 1 minute and then a 5 minute story, twice, with feedback each time.

Posture, gesture, pausing, pace and emphasis.

Questions

Quick answers.

Is there a speaker training day in Worthing?

My public days have so far been in Brighton, which is next door. Register your interest and tell me you are in Worthing, and I will let you know about dates. In-house days for teams can be held at your own premises.

Can you run a training day for my business in Worthing?

Yes. In-house days are available for teams of up to 12 (standard) or up to 30 (abridged). Get in touch to talk it through.

I have never spoken in public. Is this for me?

Yes. All levels are welcome, and the day starts with tools to calm your nerves.

What do I take away?

A story you are proud of, feedback from me and the group, a template pack and 30 days of access to an online portal.

Good to know. Coaching is coaching and education. It is not therapy or medical treatment, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can also call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.

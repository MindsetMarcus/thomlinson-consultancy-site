Questions

Quick answers.

What is the Business Health Check?

A friendly initial assessment across 12 areas of your business, from strategy and vision to cash flow and the owner's work-life balance. You receive a personalised report showing strengths, risks and opportunities, and it forms the roadmap for your coaching.

How is coaching different from consultancy?

Consultancy tends to hand you answers. Coaching helps you find and embed the answers yourself, so they fit you, your people and your business, and they stay in place when we step back.

How often do we meet, and for how long?

Sessions begin weekly and move to fortnightly after about three months, with your Private Coaching Portal alongside, over a 12 month programme.

When will I see results?

Many clients notice quick wins in the first month, with deeper change over six to twelve months. Clients have typically seen a profit uplift of at least 3 times the fees they paid. Results vary and are not guaranteed.

Is it right for my business?

It suits small and medium-sized owner-managed businesses, whether you are stabilising, scaling or preparing for sale.

What does it cost?

Fees depend on the programme and are agreed openly on a discovery call. Flexible fee deferment is available for start-ups and businesses going through a tough patch. Full details of iCan Grow are at icanthinking.com.

Good to know. Coaching is coaching and education. It is not therapy or medical treatment, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can also call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.

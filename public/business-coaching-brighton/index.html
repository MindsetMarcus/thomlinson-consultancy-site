About me and Brighton

Building the business without losing yourself.

Most owners I meet are good at what they do and short of time to work on the business instead of in it. The days fill up, the pressure builds, and the thing you started to give you freedom ends up owning you.

I know that from the inside. I spent 25 years in financial services, became an independent financial adviser and grew my business from one office to five around the country. Since then I have coached and mentored hundreds of companies, and I have run mastermind groups for owners and directors.

I have lived in and around Brighton for more than five years, I spend a day a week in the city, I go to a lot of local events and I coach a group of clients here. So when I talk about local business, I am talking about people I know.

Questions

Quick answers.

Do you meet business owners in person in Brighton?

Yes, some. I am in Brighton one day a week and meet some clients face to face, though most prefer online sessions.

What is the Business Health Check?

A friendly assessment across 12 areas of your business, with a personalised report showing strengths, risks and opportunities. It becomes the roadmap for your coaching.

Is it right for a small business?

It suits small and medium-sized owner-managed businesses, whether you are stabilising, scaling or preparing for sale.

What does it cost?

Fees depend on the programme and are agreed openly on a discovery call. Flexible fee deferment is available for start-ups and businesses going through a tough patch.

Good to know. Coaching is coaching and education. It is not therapy or medical treatment, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can also call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.

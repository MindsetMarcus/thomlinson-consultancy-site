iCan Speak

From terrified to terrific in a day.

I run iCan Speak, a one-day public speaker training course, and the days I have held in Brighton have been hugely popular. People arrive nervous and leave having delivered a story they are proud of, in a warm and supportive room.

I know that fear first hand. I was once so afraid of public speaking that I could barely breathe. I learned to turn it into a strength, went on to win more than 20 speaking competitions, and have spoken at expos, conferences and on the BBC. Now I love helping others find their voice.

A day that changes how you speak

What you will learn.

Mindset and nerves reset

Breath, body and thought drills to settle your nerves fast.

Story structure

A repeatable arc, Know, Feel, Do, that makes any message land.

Hot-seat coaching

Deliver a 1 minute and then a 5 minute story, twice, with feedback each time.

Voice and presence

Posture, gesture, pausing, pace and emphasis.

Questions

Quick answers.

Where is iCan Speak held in Brighton?

The next dates and venues are shared with my list first. Register your interest and you will hear before anyone else.

I am really nervous. Is it for me?

Yes. All levels are welcome, and the day focuses on fast-acting tools to calm your nerves and build confidence step by step.

Will I have to speak in front of everyone?

Yes, but you build up to it gradually, with coaching and practice first, in a warm and supportive room.

Can you run it for my team?

Yes. In-house days are available for teams of up to 12 (standard) or up to 30 (abridged).

Good to know. Coaching is coaching and education. It is not therapy or medical treatment, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can also call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.

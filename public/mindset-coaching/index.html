Questions

Quick answers.

What is the difference between mindset coaching and therapy?

Mindset coaching looks at where you are now and where you want to go, and builds the thinking and habits to get there. It is not therapy or medical treatment and does not replace professional help.

How quickly will I notice a change?

Most people feel a shift in their thinking early on, often within the first few weeks. Deeper, lasting change builds over time as small daily wins add up.

Is it right for me?

It is open to anyone who wants more clarity, confidence and calm, in their personal life, their work or both.

How is it different from other personal development I have tried?

Many approaches add more information. We change the mindset your thoughts come from, then back it with small daily action, a coach and a community, so the change lasts.

How much time does it take?

Sessions are typically weekly or fortnightly, plus a little time with your Private Coaching Portal and the iCan app each day. Many actions take just five minutes.

Good to know. Coaching is coaching and education. It is not therapy or medical treatment, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can also call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.

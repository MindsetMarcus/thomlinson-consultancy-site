About me and Brighton

Rooted in Brighton.

I have lived in and around Brighton for over five years. I still spend a day a week in the city, I go to a lot of events here, and I coach a group of clients here, so Brighton is not somewhere I visit. It is part of my life.

People usually come to life coaching at a turning point: a new job, a change in a relationship, a business that has outgrown them, or a quiet feeling that life could be fuller than it is. You do not need to have it all worked out. You just need to be ready to look at it honestly, and to do it with someone who is on your side.

My approach is the iCan Thinking method. We start with your thinking, because that is where your feelings and your actions begin. Then we turn what you want into small, doable actions you can see yourself taking in everyday life.

What you get

Life coaching with the iCan Thinking method.

A picture of your whole life

We begin with the Wheel of Life assessment, which looks at 12 areas of life from health and relationships to finances and self-image.

Clarity on what you want

See it, feel it, believe it. We turn a vague wish into a goal you can picture and believe in.

Small daily actions

Doable steps, many of them five minutes long, so progress builds without overwhelm.

Support between sessions

Your Private Coaching Portal keeps your goals, actions and session notes in one place.

Questions

Quick answers.

Do you meet life coaching clients in person in Brighton?

Yes, some. I am in Brighton one day a week and meet some clients face to face, though most of my Brighton clients prefer online sessions. Tell me which you would like when you book your discovery call.

I live in Hove or nearby. Does that matter?

Not at all. Online sessions work wherever you are in Brighton and Hove or the towns around, and we can arrange an in-person meeting if you would like one.

How much does life coaching in Brighton cost?

It depends on the type of coaching and how much support you want, so I go through the options with you on your free discovery call. There is no pressure to sign up.

How is life coaching different from therapy?

Coaching looks at where you are now and where you want to be, and builds the thinking and habits to get you there. It is not therapy or medical treatment and does not replace professional help.

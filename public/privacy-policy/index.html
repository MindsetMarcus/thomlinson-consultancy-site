Privacy policy

This policy explains how we look after your personal information. We keep it simple, and we only ask for what we need. Last updated: [[TODO date this policy when reviewed]].

Who we are

The Thomlinson Consultancy is the coaching and speaking practice of Marcus Thomlinson and our coaches. [[TODO confirm the legal entity that is the data controller, its registered address and its ICO registration number]]. We follow the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. Email: info@thethomlinsonconsultancy.com

What we collect

When you subscribe to our emails on this website: your first name, your email address and your marketing consent.

When you book a discovery call: the details you give our booking system, such as your name, email address and phone number.

When you get in touch or become a client: the information you share with us, and notes we keep to coach you well.

When you visit this website: our hosting provider handles basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver the pages and keep the site secure.

Why we use it, and our legal reason

To send you emails you asked for. Our reason is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

To arrange calls and provide coaching and training. Our reason is that we need it to carry out our contract with you, or to take steps you ask for before a contract.

To keep our systems secure and our records in order. Our reasons are our legitimate interests in running a safe service, and our legal duty to keep accounting records.

We never sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We use trusted providers to run this website, send emails and take bookings, and each one only receives what it needs to do its job. Cloudflare hosts and protects this website. MailerLite sends our marketing emails to the people who signed up for them. Resend sends notification emails. Our booking system handles discovery call bookings. Where they do, we make sure the transfer is covered by the protections the UK GDPR requires. We may also share information if the law requires us to.

Marketing emails

We only send marketing emails to people who ticked the box to say yes. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us at info@thethomlinsonconsultancy.com. We will stop straight away.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, so there is no cookie banner. If you use the iCan app at icanthinking.com, its own privacy policy applies.

How long we keep it

We keep client information while you work with us and for up to 12 months afterwards, unless the law says we must keep it longer. Accounting records are kept for six years. If you unsubscribe from marketing emails, we keep just enough information to make sure we do not email you again.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to ask us to correct it or delete it, to ask us to limit or object to how we use it, to ask for it in a portable format, and to withdraw your consent at any time. To use any of these rights, email info@thethomlinsonconsultancy.com. We will reply within one month.

Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the changes matter, we will tell you by email.

Questions and complaints

If you have a question or a concern, please contact us first at info@thethomlinsonconsultancy.com, and we will do our best to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

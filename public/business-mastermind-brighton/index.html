Why a mastermind

Work less, stress less, smile more.

Running a business can be lonely and relentless. Many owners tell me they are working harder than ever and enjoying it less. A mastermind gives you a small group of peers, a coach, and a weekly rhythm that keeps you moving without burning out.

I ran mastermind groups regularly before COVID, and I was one of the founding members of the Directors Hub, which began in Harrogate, North Yorkshire, as groups of directors and business owners supporting each other and later grew into a business in its own right. I would now like to run them again for local owners. I have lived in and around Brighton for more than five years, I coach a group of clients here and I go to a lot of local events, so I know the business community well enough to know that connection matters.

Questions

Quick answers.

Do you meet in person in Brighton?

That is the plan. The weekly sessions are online, and I intend each group of 12 to meet in person at least four times a year, with a quarterly meeting in Brighton and social lunches or dinners. Register your interest and I will keep you posted.

Who is the mastermind for?

Business owners and leaders who want a small group of peers, regular coaching and accountability, to grow while working less and stressing less.

How big are the groups?

Groups are capped at 12, so everyone gets time and attention.

How long is the commitment?

Business mastermind groups are a 12 month engagement, paid monthly, because that is about how long it takes to put the work in.

Good to know. Coaching is coaching and education. It is not therapy or medical treatment, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can also call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.

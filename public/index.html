Questions

Quick answers before you start.

What will working with you help me achieve?

Clarity, focus and a plan for growth, calmer and more inspiring leadership, stronger teams, healthier profits and less stress. Every programme starts with where you are now and where you want to be, and we build the path from there.

How is this different from other coaching?

We blend coaching, mentoring and training in one approach, and we go to the root: how you think. Skills matter, but mindset decides how every skill gets used, so the changes last.

Who is it for?

Successful people who want to grow, scale or step up as leaders, and anyone who senses they are capable of more and is ready to find out how much.

What results can I expect?

Clients have scaled their businesses, strengthened their teams, increased sales and found better balance. Case studies are available on request. Results vary and are not guaranteed.

How often do we meet, and what does it cost?

Sessions are usually weekly, fortnightly or monthly, with actions in between and your Private Coaching Portal to keep momentum. Fees depend on the level of support, and we agree them openly on your discovery call.

What is the link with iCan Thinking?

iCan Thinking is the method, app, courses and community created by Marcus Thomlinson, and it sits behind everything we do. The Thomlinson Consultancy is where you work with our coaches personally. You can start free at icanthinking.com at any time.

Is this therapy?

No. It is coaching and education. It helps you build calmer thinking, clearer goals and stronger habits, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.

About me and London

Working on the business, not just in it.

A London business tends to move fast and ask a lot. Clients, staff and costs all want your attention, and the work of stepping back to plan is the first thing to go.

I give that work a structure. We begin with a Business Health Check across 12 areas, then build a plan with quarterly reviews and a weekly rhythm of coaching and accountability. I have spent 25 years in business, including growing my own financial services firm from one office to five, and I have coached and mentored hundreds of companies.

I am in London one day a week meeting clients, and I am part of a large networking group and business community in the city. I also coach London owners online, which many find easiest to keep up.

Questions

Quick answers.

Do you meet in person in London?

Yes. I am in London one day a week and meet clients then, and I also coach London clients online.

I network a lot already. Do I still need a coach?

Networking brings people to you. A coach helps you decide what to do with them, and keeps you accountable for doing it.

How much time does it take?

Sessions are weekly at first, plus a few minutes between sessions in your portal. The aim is to save you time through clarity, not to add to your list.

What does it cost?

Fees depend on the programme and are agreed openly on a discovery call.

Good to know. Coaching is coaching and education. It is not therapy or medical treatment, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can also call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.

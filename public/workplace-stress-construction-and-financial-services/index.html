From me

Why I care about this.

I spent 25 years in financial services: bank assurance, investment companies, and then as an independent financial adviser, where I grew my business from one office to five around the country. Since then I have coached and trained financial services teams around the world, and helped people set up their own financial services businesses. I know the weight of targets, regulation and responsibility for other people's money.

My work with the construction industry is more recent, and it started with my builder, Alan Clayton, who has given me his permission to share this. Some years ago he was working on my house. He had a reputation as a hard man, but he was suffering from stress and anxiety at a level that was damaging his health. He wanted to protect that reputation, so it was not something he would talk about with most people. That stayed with me.

Official figures have for years shown that men working in construction trades are at higher risk of suicide than the average. A lot of men in the industry tell me they would never go to traditional sources of help, and that they feel more comfortable talking to someone who looks and sounds like me. I am 6ft 1, bald, a former rugby player, and years of karate, free fighting and rugby have left me with a slightly crooked nose. As I joke at networking events, I am not a bouncer or a bank robber.

If any of this sounds like you, or someone you work with, you do not need to be at breaking point to talk to someone. Starting earlier is almost always easier.

Source for the statement about official figures: Public Health England, analysis of Office for National Statistics data.

Free support, any time

If you need help right now.

Coaching is not an emergency service. If you are thinking about ending your life, or you are worried about someone else, please get help straight away.

  • Samaritans: call free, any time, on 116 123
  • Emergency: if there is immediate danger, call 999
  • Construction workers and their families: the Lighthouse Charity runs a free Construction Industry Helpline, open 24 hours on 0345 605 1956
  • Your GP can also help, and is a good first step for lasting worry or low mood
Mindset coaching and workplace talks

How I can help.

Using the iCan Thinking method, I work on the thinking that drives stress and anxiety at work: the voice that says you must cope alone, the habit of putting everyone else first, the belief that stopping means failing. We turn that into small, doable actions that fit around a demanding job.

You can work with me one to one, or I can bring talks and workshops on stress, resilience and mindset to your team. In both cases it is plain-spoken, practical and shaped around your day.

Questions

Quick answers.

Is this therapy or a mental health service?

No. Coaching is not therapy or medical treatment and does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or one of the services above.

I have never had coaching and I am not sure it is for someone like me. Can we just talk?

Yes. A discovery call is just a conversation, with no jargon and no pressure to continue.

What experience do you have in financial services?

I spent 25 years in financial services, in bank assurance and investment companies and then as an independent financial adviser, growing my business from one office to five. I have also coached and trained financial services teams around the world, including helping people set up their own financial services businesses.

What experience do you have in construction?

My work with the construction industry is more recent. It began when my own builder, Alan Clayton, a tough man by reputation, was suffering badly from stress and anxiety but did not feel able to talk about it. I now work with people in the industry who would not normally look for help.

Can you speak to my team or company?

Yes. I run talks and workshops on stress, resilience and mindset for teams. Get in touch to talk about what would help.

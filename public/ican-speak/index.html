Questions

Quick answers.

I'm really nervous. Is this for me?

Yes. All levels are welcome, and the day focuses on fast-acting tools to calm your nerves and build confidence step by step.

Will I have to speak in front of everyone?

Yes, but you build up to it gradually, with coaching and practice first, in a warm and supportive room.

Can I bring my own talk?

Of course. You can refine an existing talk or build one from scratch.

What feedback will I get?

Structured feedback from Marcus and the group, plus personal action points to take away.

Is there any follow-up?

VIP guests get a 60 minute one-to-one polish session within 14 days. Everyone gets 30 days of access to an online portal with recap videos and worksheets.

Can you run it for my team?

Yes. In-house days are available for teams of up to 12 people (standard) or up to 30 (abridged). Get in touch to talk it through.

Good to know. Coaching is coaching and education. It is not therapy or medical treatment, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can also call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.

About me and Worthing

Leadership that people want to follow.

Leaders do not only run businesses. They run teams, charities, committees and clubs, and the same things matter everywhere: a clear direction, a steady presence, and people who feel heard.

My Top 10 Mindsets of Great Leaders covers the thinking behind all of that, in ten short modules of about 30 minutes each. Each one comes with a practical tool and an action you can take the same day. I have led at the London 2012 Olympics, spoken at leadership conferences for organisations including Metro Bank and HSBC, and coached leaders for years.

I live in Worthing and I am active in the community, including charity events for the Lions, so I would love to support local leaders.

Questions

Quick answers.

Can we meet in person in Worthing?

Yes. I live in Worthing and meet clients here, as well as online.

I lead a charity or community group, not a company. Is this for me?

Yes. The mindsets apply wherever you lead people, whether in business, a charity or a community group.

What is the Top 10 Mindsets programme?

Ten short modules covering mindsets like pausing before reacting, owning outcomes, leading with calm and leading by serving, each with a practical tool and action.

Can you run a workshop for my team?

Yes. Custom workshops and Group Leadership Circles are available. Get in touch to talk about what would help.

Good to know. Coaching is coaching and education. It is not therapy or medical treatment, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can also call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.

Coaching is personal, so we want you to meet the people behind it. Here are our coaches, Top Trumps style: their superpower, how they got here and what they are best at.

Founder and lead coach

Marcus Thomlinson

The Calm Catalyst

SuperpowerTurning "I can't" into "I can" by changing the thinking underneath, then backing it with small daily action.

Stats

  • Mindset Mastery95
  • Calm Under Pressure90
  • Big Picture Vision92
  • Practical Action88
  • Listening and Empathy85
  • Fun Factor94

Origin story

I was running an award-winning business and running on stress, until I was diagnosed with chronic stress and told I might have 18 months left. That was my turning point. I learned to manage my mind, and iCan Thinking grew from it.

Signature move

The Calm Sigh, followed by a question that gets people seeing clearly. Calm is smooth, and smooth is fast.

Best at helping with

Stress, belief and confidence, and building a business that can run without you.

Catchphrase

Small daily wins. Big life changes.

Kryptonite

If I skip my meditation or exercise, my energy drops.

Sidekick

A good cup of tea.

Favourite iCan Thinking idea

Thoughts are what you think. Mindset is what you think from.

Fun fact

I can breathe fire, and I have won over 20 speaking competitions. I was Maritime Operations Director at the London 2012 Olympics, and I am writing my first book, The Secret Game of Life.

Origin story

I did everything I was advised to do with money, yet after years of financial problems I went bankrupt. I spent the six years on the insolvency register cleaning up my thoughts about money, and I came out knowing that our thoughts really do create our reality.

Signature move

I use a simple five-part tool called the model. This tool can be used to solve any problem and also to create any desired result.

Best at helping with

Building a great relationship with yourself, and starting and growing a service business from scratch while you are still in your corporate role.

Catchphrase

How you do one thing is how you do everything.

Kryptonite

Going to bed late. I am no good to human nor beast.

Sidekick

My Moleskine cahier journal and a blue Uniball Eye fineliner.

Fun fact

I manifested a cheque for £50,000, which arrived the day after I came off the insolvency register.

Origin story

I've been there. Stuck in anxiety, knowing there was something more I wanted yet unable to bridge the gap between knowing and doing. Anxiety and overwhelm kept the status quo firmly in place. The shift happened when I stopped fighting the feeling and began exploring it with curiosity, creating clarity, calm and balance. I now guide others across the bridge from knowing to doing. I help you turn down the noise, reframe unhelpful patterns and move from feeling stuck to living your life the way you desire.

Signature move

Best at helping with

Catchphrase

Kryptonite

Sidekick

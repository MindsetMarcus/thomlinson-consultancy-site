The method

Change the way you think, and you change what you can do.

iCan Thinking is the positive-thinking method created by Marcus Thomlinson. It is the heart of every session we run, and it is simple enough to start using today.

The four steps

See it. Feel it. Believe it. Do it.

Wishful thinking hopes. iCan thinking adds belief that is backed by action, and that is what makes the difference.

Step one

See it.

Picture the outcome you want, clearly and in colour. A vision board in the iCan app keeps your future vivid.

Step two

Feel it.

Connect with why it matters until you feel it in every fibre. Emotion is the fuel that moves you.

Step three

Believe it.

Let the belief land, so your goals are truly yours. This is where wishing becomes knowing.

Step four

Do it, every day.

Tiny actions, tracked and celebrated, add up fast. Streaks and Great Wins make progress visible.

A different way to think

Thoughts are what you think. Mindset is what you think from.

Most of us try to change our results by working harder at what we do. iCan Thinking starts one level deeper, with the mindset your thoughts come from. When that shifts, your thoughts, feelings and actions shift with it, and results follow without the struggle.

It is not the same as positive thinking. Positive thinking on its own can feel like wishful thinking. iCan thinking adds belief backed by action, using See it, Feel it, Believe it and then doing it every day.

You do not have to rely on motivation or willpower. You build identity, structure and consistency through small daily wins, and those wins add up to big life changes.

Your toolkit

Simple tools you can use today.

The Calm Sigh

A simple breath that settles your mind in the moment, so you can respond with thought rather than reaction.

Mindset Mantras

Short phrases that reset your thinking in seconds when you need it most.

Delete That Programme

A practical tool for letting go of limiting beliefs and installing better ones.

Great Wins

Log your wins as they happen. Seeing how far you have come is powerful.

Goals and milestones

Break every goal into dated actions, with reminders and a picture to keep it alive.

Vision board and journal

Keep your future in view and notice what is going well, day by day.

The goal formula

SFB = A².

See it, Feel it, Believe it equals Achievement squared. It is the simple formula behind goals that stick, and you will use it from your first session.

About me and London

Calm, clear leadership in a demanding city.

London leaders carry a lot: targets, people, stakeholders and a diary that never empties. In that environment it is easy to react, and a reacting leader tends to create reacting teams.

My Top 10 Mindsets of Great Leaders is built for busy people: ten modules of about 30 minutes, each with a mindset, a tool and an action. I have spoken at leadership conferences for organisations including Metro Bank and HSBC, and I have led as Maritime Operations Director at the London 2012 Olympics.

I am in London one day a week, and part of a large networking group and business community in the city, so I know how fast things move here.

Questions

Quick answers.

Do you work with leaders in person in London?

Yes, on my London day each week. I also coach London leaders online.

Can you run leadership workshops for my team?

Yes. Group Leadership Circles and custom workshops are available for teams, and I can come to you.

I have little spare time. Is this realistic?

Yes. It is designed to save you time through clarity and focus. The programme modules are about 30 minutes each.

What results can I expect?

Many leaders report calmer decisions, stronger teams and less stress. Results vary and are not guaranteed.

Good to know. Coaching is coaching and education. It is not therapy or medical treatment, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can also call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.

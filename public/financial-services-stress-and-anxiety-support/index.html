From me

Why I understand.

I spent 25 years in financial services: bank assurance, investment companies, and then as an independent financial adviser, where I grew my business from one office to five around the country. Since then I have coached and trained financial services teams around the world, and helped people set up their own financial services businesses.

I know the weight of targets, regulation and responsibility for other people's money. It is a world that prizes confidence and results, and that can make it hard to admit when you are struggling.

Stress and anxiety are rarely a sign of weakness. More often they show how much you care and how much you carry. You do not need to be at breaking point to talk to someone. Starting earlier is almost always easier.

Coaching and therapy

It is not where you start, it is where you end up.

The joy of coaching is that it looks forward. It does not matter where you are starting from. What matters is where you are now and where you want to get to.

Therapy looks back, at what has happened and how it has affected you. For the people who need it, it is important and often essential, and if you are struggling I would always encourage you to speak to your GP or a qualified therapist. Coaching is a different kind of help. It is not therapy and it does not replace it.

What coaching does is retrain your brain and turn your attention to the future. Using the iCan Thinking method, we change the way you think, one small step at a time, so you can start living in the world you want to live in and build the future you truly want, rather than looking backwards all the time. That is where hope comes from: knowing you can change what happens next.

Signs it may be time to talk

You might recognise this.

  • Lying awake going over the day's decisions
  • Dreading the inbox, the next target or the next review
  • Feeling you have to cope on your own
  • Working harder and enjoying it less
  • Keeping up appearances while the pressure builds
Free support, any time

If you need help right now.

Coaching is not an emergency service. If you are thinking about ending your life, or you are worried about someone else, please get help straight away.

  • Samaritans: call free, any time, on 116 123
  • Emergency: if there is immediate danger, call 999
  • Your GP can also help, and is a good first step for lasting worry or low mood
Mindset coaching and talks

How I can help.

Using the iCan Thinking method, I work on the thinking that drives stress and anxiety at work: the voice that says you must cope alone, the habit of putting everyone else first, the belief that stopping means failing. We turn that into small, doable steps towards the life and career you want.

You can work with me one to one, or I can bring talks and workshops on stress, resilience and mindset to your team or firm. I meet clients in person in Worthing, Brighton and London, and I coach online anywhere.

Questions

Quick answers.

Is this therapy or a mental health service?

No. Therapy looks back at the past and is vital for people who need it. Coaching looks forward, helping you build the future you want. It is not therapy or medical treatment and does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or one of the services listed on this page.

What experience do you have in financial services?

I spent 25 years in financial services, in bank assurance and investment companies and then as an independent financial adviser, growing my business from one office to five. I have also coached and trained financial services teams around the world, including helping people set up their own financial services businesses.

I have never had coaching and I am not sure it is for someone like me. Can we just talk?

Yes. A discovery call is just a conversation, with no jargon and no pressure to continue.

Will anyone at my firm know I have spoken to you?

Not from me. Whether you tell anyone is your choice, and a first conversation commits you to nothing.

Can you speak to my team or firm?

Yes. I run talks and workshops on stress, resilience and mindset for teams. Get in touch to talk about what would help.

Good to know. Coaching is coaching and education. It is not therapy or medical treatment, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can also call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.

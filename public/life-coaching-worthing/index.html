About me and Worthing

A local coach in Worthing.

I live in Worthing, and I have been getting involved in the community here, including a number of charity events for the Lions. I enjoy being part of a place, and I would like to help more people in and around Worthing feel clearer and more confident about the life they are building.

Life coaching is for anyone who feels there is more in them than they are currently living. It might be confidence, stress, direction, relationships, or a sense that you are always busy but not quite moving towards what matters. We look at all of it, not just the part that feels most urgent.

I use the iCan Thinking method. It starts with how you think, because your thoughts shape your feelings and your actions, then helps you turn your goals into small steps you can take every day. Many of those steps take only five minutes.

What you get

Life coaching with the iCan Thinking method.

A picture of your whole life

We begin with the Wheel of Life assessment, which looks at 12 areas of life from health and relationships to finances and self-image.

Clarity on what you want

See it, feel it, believe it. We turn a vague wish into a goal you can picture and believe in.

Small daily actions

Doable steps, many of them five minutes long, so progress builds without overwhelm.

Support between sessions

Your Private Coaching Portal keeps your goals, actions and session notes in one place.

Questions

Quick answers.

Can we meet in person in Worthing?

Yes. I live in Worthing and meet clients here in person, as well as online. We agree a time and place that works for you when you book your discovery call.

Do I have to be in Worthing to work with you?

No. Online sessions work from anywhere, so you can work with me from Worthing, from elsewhere in West Sussex, or from further away.

What happens on the free discovery call?

We talk about where you are, where you would like to be and whether coaching with me feels like the right fit. You leave with a clearer picture either way, and there is no pressure to go further.

Is life coaching right for me if I am not sure what my goal is?

Yes. Many people start with just a feeling that something needs to change. Working out what you want is part of the coaching, and we begin with a simple look at the different areas of your life.

Good to know. Coaching is coaching and education. It is not therapy or medical treatment, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can also call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.

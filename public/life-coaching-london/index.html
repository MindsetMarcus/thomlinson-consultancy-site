About me and London

Coaching that fits a London life.

I travel to London one day a week to meet clients, and I am part of a large networking group and business community in the city. Many of the people I coach here have demanding work, a busy diary and very little spare headspace.

That is usually when coaching helps most. When life is full, it is easy to react to the next demand and lose sight of what you actually want. Together we step back, look at your life as a whole, and decide what matters most, then build a simple plan you can follow without adding to your overload.

I use the iCan Thinking method, which starts with your thinking and turns your goals into small daily actions. Because the actions are small, they fit around a London schedule rather than competing with it.

Questions

Quick answers.

Do you meet clients in person in London?

Yes, I am in London one day a week and meet clients face to face then. I also coach London clients online, which many people find easier to fit around work.

I live outside London but work there. Can we still work together?

Yes. Online sessions mean it does not matter where you live, and if you would like to meet in person we can see whether my London day suits you.

Is the coaching suitable for busy people?

It is designed for them. The actions we agree are small, many take only five minutes, and your Private Coaching Portal keeps your goals and actions in one place between sessions.

What does a free discovery call involve?

A friendly conversation about where you are, where you want to be and whether we are a good fit. There is no obligation to continue.

Good to know. Coaching is coaching and education. It is not therapy or medical treatment, and it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional. In the UK you can also call Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123.
